Our Story

A small bakery with unreasonable standards

The Crumb Atelier started in a home kitchen in 2020, selling sourdough to neighbours over WhatsApp. The rules haven't changed since: real butter, slow fermentation, no premixes, no "cake flavour" anything. If it can't be made properly, it doesn't go on the counter.

Our ovens fire up at 5 AM so the first croissants crackle at eight. By noon, most of the counter is a memory — pre-ordering is not a luxury here, it's strategy.
